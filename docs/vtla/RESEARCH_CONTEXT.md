# Research Context

This document records public research context for the extension. It is not evidence of affiliation or endorsement.

## ExPLoRe open question

The ExPLoRe author publicly described generalization of dispatch-weight loss coupling outside masked image modeling as the main open question, identifying multimodal pretraining as a promising direction:

https://www.gkos.dev/blog/per-patch-loss-coupling

## Amazon Robotics public research topics

Amazon's Spring 2026 robotics call publicly listed areas that overlap the evaluation surface of ExPLoRe-VTLA:

- multimodal perception and task grounding
- force and tactile sensing
- contact-rich manipulation and compliant control
- multimodal world models
- causal reasoning about contact dynamics
- cross-embodiment transfer
- synthetic data and data quality
- safety-critical control, verification, and monitoring

Source:

https://www.amazon.science/research-awards/robotics-call-for-proposals-spring-2026

Amazon's 2026 GAM publication is also relevant as evidence that cross-embodiment VLA manipulation is a production-relevant research direction. ExPLoRe-VTLA does not claim equivalence to GAM or access to Amazon's proprietary data or robot fleet.

https://www.amazon.science/publications/gam-generalized-action-model-for-robotic-manipulation

## Public data landscape

Daimon-Infinity publicly describes a large vision-tactile-language-action dataset with released tactile and robot-state streams:

https://github.com/dmrobot-admin/Daimon-Infinity

FreeTacMan provides public visuo-tactile contact-rich manipulation research code/data tooling:

https://github.com/OpenDriveLab/FreeTacMan

DROID provides a large in-the-wild robot manipulation dataset and training resources:

https://droid-dataset.github.io/

These references motivate adapter targets. They are not included results.
